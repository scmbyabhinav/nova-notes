Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ORAH Notes ("ORAH", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the ORAH Notes Android application. It describes the app's intended data practices and should be read alongside the permissions and disclosures shown in the app and on Google Play.

1. Information in your notes

You may create notes, checklists, and other content in ORAH. This content may contain personal or sensitive information that you choose to enter. The app uses device storage and the app's enabled storage features to handle your content. Where you use sharing, export, backup, or restore features, the information involved may be processed by the selected feature or service.

Do not enter information into the app that you are not comfortable storing or sharing through the features you choose to use.

2. Analytics and diagnostic information

The app project includes anonymous analytics dependencies. Depending on the version and configuration you use, the app may process app-usage events, technical information, or diagnostic information to understand performance and improve the app. The precise information collected, the analytics provider, and whether information is transmitted off your device depend on the analytics implementation enabled in that version.

Before publishing this policy, the developer must confirm the analytics SDKs actually enabled, the data they collect, their recipients, and any available opt-out controls. This policy does not mean that analytics are necessarily enabled or that collected information is necessarily anonymous in every circumstance.

3. Purchases and subscriptions

ORAH may offer optional paid features or subscriptions through Google Play Billing. If you make a purchase, Google Play processes the transaction under its own terms and privacy policy. ORAH may receive purchase or entitlement information needed to verify access to paid features, manage subscriptions, or restore eligible purchases. ORAH does not ask you to provide your full payment-card details directly in the app.

4. Sharing and exports

If you choose to share, export, or send content, the selected Android feature, app, recipient, or service may receive the content you choose to share. Review the destination before sharing. The privacy practices of third-party services are governed by those services' own policies.

5. How information is used

Information handled by ORAH may be used, where applicable, to:

  • Provide note-taking and organization features.
  • Save and retrieve content and preferences.
  • Support purchases and access to eligible premium features.
  • Diagnose technical issues and improve reliability.
  • Respond to support requests and comply with legal obligations.

6. Storage, security, and retention

We use the storage and security mechanisms implemented in the current app version. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. How long information remains available depends on the type of information, the app's storage behavior, your device, and any services or exports you use.

If you uninstall the app, reset your device, or delete app data, locally stored content may be lost. Exported files, backups, and information held by third-party services may remain separately. Keep your own backup of important information where supported.

7. Permissions

Depending on the features you use, ORAH may request Android permissions for functions such as microphone-based voice input, access to selected media or files, or other app features. Permissions should be requested when needed. You can review available permission controls in your Android device settings. Actual permissions depend on the installed version.

8. Third-party services

ORAH may rely on third-party services for distribution, billing, analytics, or features you choose to use. Those providers may process information according to their own policies. The developer should identify the providers actually enabled in the released app and link to their applicable privacy notices here where required.

Google Play privacy information: https://policies.google.com/privacy

9. Children's privacy

ORAH is a general-purpose note-taking app and is not specifically designed for children. The developer does not knowingly seek to collect children's personal information in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a feature that transmits it to the developer, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

10. Your choices and rights

You can choose what content you enter and whether to use optional sharing or export features. Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information processed by the developer. Contact us using the details below to make a privacy request. Requests concerning Google Play purchases or third-party services may need to be directed to those providers.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as ORAH's features, services, or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published at this webpage, with its effective date updated when appropriate.
