# ORAH Google Play Billing Setup

## Product IDs

The app code uses these non-consumable purchase product IDs:

| Plan | Product ID | App entitlement |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly | `orah_pro_monthly` | Pro Monthly |
| Yearly | `orah_pro_yearly` | Pro Yearly |
| Lifetime | `orah_pro_lifetime` | Pro Lifetime |

The IDs above are source-code identifiers. They do **not** mean the products
have already been created or activated in Google Play Console.

## Play Console work still required

1. Create the three products in the monetization area for the ORAH app.
2. Use the exact product IDs above.
3. Set the desired regional prices in Play Console.
4. Configure the subscription billing periods for Monthly and Yearly.
5. Configure the Lifetime product as a one-time purchase.
6. Add testers and verify purchase, restore, cancellation and expiry behavior
   on an internal test track.
7. Confirm the app package/application ID generated by the Android host matches
   the Play Console app before publishing.

## Important billing limitation

The current client implementation restores purchases and applies a cached
entitlement locally. It deliberately documents that production-grade
subscription verification should eventually use Google Play server-side
verification. Do not treat the client-side expiry calculation as a security
boundary for a high-value subscription.

## Pricing

Prices are intentionally not hard-coded in the app. The store supplies the
localized price shown on the ORAH Pro screen, so pricing can be changed in
Play Console without shipping a new APK/AAB.
